Aesthetic Surgery

A natural, balanced result starts with precise planning. Dr. Topaz personally accompanies every patient, from the first consultation through full recovery.

Procedures

What does Dr. Topaz operate on?

Every operation is planned individually according to your anatomy, age, medical condition and expectations. At the consultation you will receive a detailed, honest and realistic explanation of the options, the procedure and the recovery process.

👁

Eyelid Surgery (Blepharoplasty)

Removal of excess skin and fat from the upper and lower eyelids, restoring a fresh, younger look and improving the visual field in cases of drooping eyelids. A precise, delicate procedure, mostly under local anesthesia, with relatively quick recovery.

֎

Rhinoplasty

Reshaping the nose in full harmony with your facial features, while preserving normal breathing function. Dr. Topaz emphasizes a natural, harmonious result, not an "operated" nose.

✦

Facial Rejuvenation & Facelift

Surgical treatment of signs of aging in the face and neck: lifting sagging tissues, repositioning contour lines and restoring vitality, with a conservative approach that preserves natural expression.

◈

Breast Surgery

Breast augmentation with implants, breast reduction and breast lift. As founder and chair of the International Breast Implant Registry (IBIR), Dr. Topaz brings unique global knowledge of implant safety and long-term follow-up.

◍

Removal of Skin Lesions & Tumors

Excision of moles, lesions, lipomas and benign or malignant skin tumors, combined with meticulous plastic closure that minimizes scarring, including in delicate areas such as the face and nose.

✚

Combined & Complex Cases

His deep reconstructive experience allows Dr. Topaz to take on challenging cases: revision surgery, correction of results of previous operations, and patients with complex medical backgrounds.

FAQ

Before you decide

How do I choose a plastic surgeon?

Verify that the physician is a certified specialist in plastic surgery with proven experience in the specific procedure you are considering, and that you feel comfortable asking any question. Seniority, academic activity and international professional recognition are good indicators of professional level.

Will the result look natural?

That is exactly Dr. Topaz's approach: a balanced, harmonious result that respects your natural appearance. At the consultation you will receive a realistic assessment of the expected result, without exaggerated promises.

How long is the recovery?

Recovery time varies by procedure: from a few days for eyelid surgery to several weeks for breast surgery or a facelift. In every case you will receive detailed instructions and personal follow-up throughout the process.

Where are the operations performed?

Operations are performed in certified operating rooms at leading private hospitals, with an experienced anesthesia team. Consultations and follow-up take place at the Ra'anana clinic.

I am debating between several options. What should I do?

Come for a consultation. An important part of Dr. Topaz's role is to help you decide what is right for you, including the option of not operating at all. Professional, honest advice comes before any decision.
