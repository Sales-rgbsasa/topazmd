Lectures & Speaking

Three decades of clinical struggle and research, distilled into lectures that challenge dogmas and change practice. Available for conferences, hospitals, universities and industry events worldwide.

Invited Lecturer & Keynote Speaker

A speaker who has been in the operating room the morning of the talk

Dr. Topaz lectures from live clinical experience: thousands of surgeries, decades of research, and technologies he invented that are used by surgeons around the world. His talks combine hard data, real cases and a willingness to challenge accepted dogmas in surgical infection management.

Lecture Topics

Core speaking themes

⇤⇥

Closure of Hard-to-Heal & Complex Wounds

Primary closure of large, high-tension wounds using stress relaxation and mechanical creep; the TopClosure® approach; Regulated Oxygen-enriched and Irrigation-assisted Negative Pressure Therapy (ROINPT); avoiding grafts and flaps; applications in trauma, military and mass-casualty settings.

✚

Management of Wound Infections

Challenging antibiotic strategies and current dogmas in surgical infections: ultra-high concentration local antibiotic delivery, antibiotic-resistant bacteria, sternal infections after cardiac surgery, and conservative salvage of infected cardiac implantable devices.

◈

Breast Implant Safety & Registries

Lessons from founding and chairing the International Breast Implant Registry (IBIR): long-term implant safety, quality assurance and international standards in aesthetic surgery.

💡

From Bedside Problem to Medical Device

The journey of a surgeon-inventor: identifying unmet clinical needs, developing and validating new medical technologies, and bringing them from concept to worldwide clinical use.
