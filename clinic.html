Getting Here

Directions, parking & accessibility

🚗 From Route 4 Southbound

Take the Ra'anana North / Kfar Saba exit, bear right onto Weizmann Street, and at the first traffic light turn right onto HaTidhar Street.

🚗 From Route 4 Northbound

At the Ra'anana North junction take the exit toward Kfar Saba / Ra'anana, keeping left for Ra'anana. At the second traffic light (Weizmann Street) turn right into HaTidhar Street.

🅿 Parking

Paid public parking is available on HaTidhar Street, adjacent to the building.

♿ Accessibility

The building and the clinic are wheelchair accessible. Patients using a wheelchair are asked to notify the staff in advance, so we can arrange entry through the building's parking lot.

🚌 Public Transport

Many bus lines stop on Weizmann Street, a short walk from the clinic.

📋 What to Bring

Relevant medical documents, a list of regular medications and recent test results if available. Every document helps us give you the most precise advice.
