Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This document briefly describes what information is collected on this site and how we use it.

What information is collected?

The only information collected on this site is what you choose to provide in the contact form: name, phone, email and the content of your inquiry. We do not collect medical information through the site, and we recommend not detailing sensitive medical information in the form; medical information will be requested only at the consultation itself.

How is the information used?

Inquiry details are used solely to contact you and arrange a meeting. The information is not passed to third parties, is not sold and is not used for marketing mailings.

Security & retention

The site is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Form submissions are stored in a secure system and deleted after the inquiry is handled.

Your rights

In accordance with the Israeli Privacy Protection Law, you may request to review, correct or delete the information you provided. For any request, contact us at +972-9-7463737.
