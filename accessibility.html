Accessibility Statement

This website was built with a commitment to allow the entire public, including people with disabilities, comfortable and equal browsing, in accordance with the Israeli Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Law and Israeli Standard 5568, at level AA.

Accessibility features of the site

  • Semantic page structure and hierarchical headings supporting screen readers
  • Full keyboard navigation, including a skip-to-content link and clear focus indication
  • Alternative text for images and color contrast meeting the standard
  • Support for browser text enlargement and a responsive layout for every screen size
  • Reduced animations for users who set a reduced-motion preference

Clinic accessibility

The clinic at 16 HaTidhar St., Ra'anana, is wheelchair accessible. Patients using a wheelchair are asked to notify the staff in advance, so a comfortable entry through the building's parking lot can be arranged.

Encountered a problem?

We work to improve the site's accessibility continually. If you have encountered a difficulty or have a suggestion, please contact us at +972-9-7463737 or through the contact form, and we will handle it promptly.
