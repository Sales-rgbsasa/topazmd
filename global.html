Global Program

Bringing the TopClosure®, ROINPT and CITA protocol to institutions, referring physicians and international patients.

For Institutions & Referring Physicians

A five-part implementation program

Dr. Topaz works with hospitals and academic centers worldwide through a structured program for adopting the integrated protocol.

1

Scientific Lectures

Evidence-based lectures on the mechanism, indications and clinical experience of the integrated protocol.

2

Case-Based Multidisciplinary Teaching

Joint case discussions with surgery, cardiology, infectious disease, rehabilitation and nursing teams.

3

Hands-On Workshops

Practical training in TopClosure® application, negative-pressure management and CITA delivery.

4

Proctored Clinical Implementation

On-site guidance of the first cases, together with the local team.

5

Collaborative Outcome Evaluation

Joint follow-up, outcome analysis and scientific publication with the adopting center.

Resource-Limited Settings

Technology that travels well

An independent single-center series from Nairobi, Kenya (Chege et al., Pan Afr J Plast Reconstr Aesthet Surg, 2026) reported flap- and graft-free closure of complex defects using the TopClosure® tension-relief system, at a device cost roughly 23-fold lower than microvascular free-tissue transfer: direct support for international dissemination of these technologies where reconstructive infrastructure is limited.

For International Patients

Treatment in Israel

International patient procedures are currently performed at Herzliya Medical Center, Israel. Consultation requests are welcome for infected cardiac devices (CIED), deep tissue pressure injuries, diabetic wounds and infected amputations, open abdomen, infected sternal wounds, and complex trauma wounds.
