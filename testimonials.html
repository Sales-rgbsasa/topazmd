From Patients

Thank-you letters

Patient thank-you letters published through the official channels of the Hillel Yaffe Medical Center.

"Since the year 2000 I have been treated by Dr. Topaz, who accompanied me with devotion through a series of operations: removal of skin tumors from the nose with skin grafts, operations on my left hand and on the fingers of my right hand. Although I was entitled to surgery through my health fund, I chose again and again to be operated on by Dr. Topaz, thanks to his loyal, professional and humane care and that of the devoted team at his side."

R.G., Petah TikvaThank-you letter to the management of the Hillel Yaffe Medical Center, December 2010

Over his 26 years as head of the Plastic Surgery Unit at the Hillel Yaffe Medical Center, Dr. Topaz received many thank-you letters from patients and their families, some published on the hospital's official website. The letters repeat the same motifs: uncompromising professionalism, personal devotion and availability for every question.

From the World

International honors & recognition

🇨🇳

Recognition from the Premier of China

For volunteering to treat casualties of the devastating 2008 earthquake in Sichuan Province. His work led to a first-of-its-kind sister-hospital agreement between Israel and China, signed in the presence of Israel's ambassador.

🇬🇧

Review by NICE (UK)

The TopClosure® system invented by Dr. Topaz was reviewed by the UK's National Institute for Health and Care Excellence in its Medtech Innovation Briefing series (MIB97).

🇮🇳

Ethicon Visiting Professorship

The Association of Plastic Surgeons of India awarded Dr. Topaz its prestigious Ethicon Visiting Professorship in Cosmetic Surgery.

🏥

The Trust of Leading Hospitals

Dr. Topaz is invited to operate as a senior volunteer surgeon at Sheba (Tel Hashomer), Ichilov, Hillel Yaffe and Shaare Zedek: testimony to the professional trust of Israel's medical community.

🎓

Visiting Professor in China

Appointed Visiting Professor at university hospitals in China, including the People's Hospital of Deyang in Sichuan and the Shihezi University hospital.

In the Press

"Israel Hayom" (2012) told how the device Dr. Topaz developed for treating casualties of hostile acts was also enlisted to treat injured animals: testimony to the creativity and the big heart behind the medicine.
